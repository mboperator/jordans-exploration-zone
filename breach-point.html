how a round works

Attacking — you spawn with the breacher. Reach the site, hold F for 4s to plant, then survive 45s. Wiping the defenders before the plant also wins the round.
Defending — stop the plant, or defuse it in 7s once it's down. Wiping the attackers wins.
Credits carry between rounds. Buy the good gun.
